1. Who We Are

Seasonally ("Seasonally," "we," "us," or "our") is a personal finance application built for seasonal and blue-collar workers. We are operated by Fugate Leasing and Consulting, a Indiana limited liability company, with a principal place of business at 5805 W 10th Street, Indianapolis, IN 46224.

2. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy describes how Seasonally collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our mobile application (the "App") and our marketing website at getseasonally.com (together with the App, the "Services"). By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide Directly

Account information: your name, email address, and password when you create an account.

Profile information: your pay type (hourly or salaried), work category and subcategory, state of work, and zip code (optional).

Savings goals: your seasonal fund target, savings preferences, and any manually entered savings amounts.

Debt information you add manually: account type, current balance, interest rate, and minimum payment for debts you choose to track manually rather than through Plaid.

Category preferences: your Required vs. Extra spending classifications and merchant-level overrides.

Partner invitations: an email address or invitation code you provide to invite a household partner.

Optional photo: a profile photo you may upload, stored in our cloud storage and visible only within your account.

Information from social sign-in: if you choose to sign in with Facebook, we receive your name and email address from Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook) to create or sign in to your Seasonally account. You may sign in with email and password instead at any time. We do not post to Facebook, read your Facebook contacts, or receive any other Facebook profile data.

Information from social sign-in with Google: if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google LLC to create or sign in to your Seasonally account. You may sign in with email and password instead at any time. We do not access your Google Drive, contacts, or any other Google account data.

3.2 Information We Collect Through Plaid

When you connect your bank accounts through Plaid, we receive the following information through Plaid's read-only API:

  • Account metadata: account names, account types (checking, savings, credit card, loan), the last four digits of account numbers (account "mask"), and institution identifiers.
  • Transaction history: merchant names, transaction amounts, dates, and Plaid-assigned spending categories.
  • Balance information: current and available account balances.
  • Income and paycheck deposits.
  • Liabilities data (when supported by your financial institution): credit card balances and APRs, loan principal balances and interest rates, minimum payment amounts, and statement balances. This information powers the debt tracking features described in Section 6.4.
  • Important: Seasonally has read-only access to your bank data. We cannot and do not initiate transfers, move money, or access your banking credentials. Your bank login credentials are entered directly into Plaid's secure interface and are never transmitted to or stored by Seasonally.

3.3 Information Generated by the App

Spending categorizations and insights generated from your transaction data.

Seasonal fund calculations, savings pace metrics, and runway estimates.

Daily snapshots of your debt account balances (used to detect changes over time, such as growing credit card balances; described further in Section 6.4).

Lesson progress, streaks, XP, and levels within the financial education curriculum.

App usage data including features accessed and settings preferences.

3.4 Technical Information

Device information: device type, operating system version, app version, and unique device identifiers.

Log data: app crashes, performance data, and error reports.

Push notification tokens for delivering app notifications.

Over-the-air update telemetry: the version of the App you are running and the success or failure of update downloads (delivered via Expo).

5.2 Partner Access (Household Sharing Feature)

If you choose to connect with a household partner through the App, your partner will have read-only visibility into your shared household dashboard, including combined income, combined required expenses, and combined net. Your partner can only see data from accounts you explicitly choose to share. You can revoke partner access at any time through Account settings.

If you and your partner have both linked the same joint bank account, the App will detect this match using the last four digits of the account number and the institution identifier, and will display the account only once in your household view to prevent double-counting. This match is performed within our own infrastructure and does not involve any third party.

6. How Specific Features Use Your Data

6.1 Seasonal Fund and Pace Calculations

Your seasonal fund target, pace progress, and runway estimates are calculated entirely on our own infrastructure using your transaction history, income detections, and Required-vs-Extra categorizations. These calculations do not involve any AI provider or third party beyond Plaid (which provides the underlying transaction data) and Supabase (which hosts our database).

6.2 Spending Insights

Spending insights — such as "your gas spending is up 25% this month" — are generated using deterministic rules running on our own infrastructure. The underlying math never leaves Supabase. Where AI is used to generate the narrative copy for an insight, the data sent to the AI provider is limited to aggregated category totals and metadata as described in Section 7.

6.3 Financial Education and Streak Tracking

Lesson progress, streaks, XP, and levels are stored in your account and are not shared with any third party. They are not used for advertising or sold.

6.4 Debt Tracking and Distress Monitoring

Seasonally offers a debt tracking feature that helps you see your credit card and loan balances, interest rates, and minimum payments in one place, and projects payoff timelines based on payment amounts you choose to apply.

To provide this feature, we collect liability data through Plaid (when your financial institution supports it) and store daily snapshots of your debt account balances. These snapshots are used to detect changes over time — for example, a credit card balance that grows steadily over multiple months.

We run a background scan once per day that evaluates these snapshots and other transaction signals for patterns that may indicate financial distress, including: credit card balances growing over time, recurring high-fee short-term lending transactions, multiple buy-now-pay-later commitments, and minimum payments that exceed a significant portion of your income.

If our scan identifies such a pattern, the App may surface a contextually relevant educational lesson in your Home tab. The App does not assign you a score, label you, or share this information with any third party.

In addition, when one of these patterns is detected, an internal notification is sent to the Seasonally founding team so that a human can review the situation and, in limited cases, reach out directly to the affected user with offers of additional Lite Mode access or other support. This outreach is conducted by Seasonally personnel only, is not automated marketing, and is not shared with any third party.

6.5 Local Resources (211 National Data Platform)

Seasonally includes a Local Resources feature that can show you nearby food banks, utility assistance programs, medical assistance, and similar resources. When you use this feature, the App sends your zip code and a category keyword (such as "food bank" or "utility assistance") to the 211 National Data Platform, operated by United Way Worldwide, in order to retrieve locally relevant results.

We do not send your name, email address, account information, financial data, or any other personally identifiable information to 211. The lookup is performed only when you actively use the feature. Your zip code is also stored within your Seasonally account for convenience.

7. AI Features and Data Handling

Seasonally uses Anthropic's Claude API to power several features, including: a monthly spending summary, on-demand explanations of individual spending insights, onboarding personalization, household empathy responses (when income is below required expenses), fund milestone messages, and a weekly insight digest. All AI features use only anonymized, aggregate data.

Specifically, we send to the Anthropic API:

  • Total spending amounts by category (e.g., "Housing: $1,627") — not individual transactions.
  • Total income for the period.
  • Net spending result.
  • Aggregate fund progress percentages and pace status.
  • Generic profile context such as work category and state (used to tailor wording, never combined with identifying information).

We do not send to the Anthropic API:

  • Raw transaction descriptions or merchant names.
  • Your name, email, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • Your bank account numbers or Plaid access tokens.
  • Specific debt account balances, account masks, or institution identifiers.
  • Anthropic does not use data submitted through the API to train its AI models. Anthropic retains API request logs for up to thirty (30) days for abuse detection and security purposes only, and deletes them thereafter.

8. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase's managed PostgreSQL infrastructure in AWS us-east-1 (United States). We implement the following security measures:

  • Row Level Security (RLS) enforced at the database level — you can only access your own data, and partner sharing is enforced by database-level policies.
  • All API keys and third-party credentials are stored server-side only and are never embedded in the mobile application bundle.
  • Plaid access tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-GCM encryption with a server-side key.
  • All data transmission between the App and our servers, and between our servers and our subprocessors, uses TLS encryption.
  • Plaid webhooks are verified using Plaid's signed JWT mechanism before any state change is applied.
  • Payment card data is handled exclusively by Stripe's hosted infrastructure — we never receive or store card numbers.
  • No system is perfectly secure. While we use industry-standard practices to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you without unreasonable delay and in accordance with applicable law.

9. Data Retention

We retain your account data and transaction history for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data from our active production systems within thirty (30) days, except where we are required to retain it for legal compliance, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution purposes.

Backup systems may retain data for an additional period not to exceed ninety (90) days before secure deletion in the ordinary course.

If you enter Lite Mode, your bank accounts remain connected and your data continues to sync as normal. The only change in Lite Mode is that subscription billing pauses. All app features, insights, and transaction data remain fully active during Lite Mode.
